Click a staff name to add or edit a holiday date range for that person. Click an existing orange cell to edit or remove it. The coverage row under the dates shows whether the staff still available that day — and eligible for the posts that need filling — can actually cover everything: green/amber = spare capacity, red = a real shortfall (some post can't be filled by anyone available, even in the best arrangement). Staff with no weekly hours cap set, or on holiday, aren't counted as available. Weeks run Friday → Thursday, matching the roster.
